# StageKit 0.1.0-beta180

A capture could stay in use after its scene was left part-way through a change.

## Fixed

- **A capture no longer stays in use when you come back to a scene you left during a change.**
  Suppose a capture's show or hide transition was still running when you switched away with a cut,
  and the capture was then shown and hidden again while that scene was away. When you came back,
  the old transition was picked up again, and OBS counted the hidden capture as in use. A capture
  card set to let go of its device could then open it and close it again. Every show or hide that
  Link Visibility, Show in Scenes or Scene Modes makes now ends what is left of the capture's earlier change. It used to depend on Link
  Visibility's twice-a-second check catching it in time.

## Good to know

- Link Visibility's log line *ended a transition left under way* now also names the scene.
- Not yet tried on Windows.
