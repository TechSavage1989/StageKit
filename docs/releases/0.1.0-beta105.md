# StageKit 0.1.0-beta105

One hundred and fifth pre-release: a fitted item can be in the same place in every scene before you
switch to it. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## New

- **Fit to Space: *Same place in other scenes*** (unticked as it comes). Each time the filter has
  put its item in place in the scene on screen, the same source in your other scenes is given
  exactly that place and size. Switch scenes and it is already there: nothing shifts in view.
  - Scenes that are on screen at that moment are left alone.
  - Locked copies are moved too.
  - A scene with a Fit to Space of its own for the item fits it to that scene when it comes into
    view, at once and without a glide. Where your scenes are laid out alike, that is the same
    place.

## Please check

1. Tick *Same place in other scenes* on the Fit to Space filters of the items you want linked
   (the game, the chat box, the camera), in each scene that has the filter.
2. Hide the camera in one scene so the others move, then switch scenes: are they already in place?
