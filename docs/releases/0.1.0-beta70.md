# StageKit 0.1.0-beta70

Seventieth pre-release: Fit Item can fill the free space around an item, keeping an exact gap from
the screen's edges and from other items. It passes the automated tests on Linux; **it has not been
run in OBS on Windows yet.**

## New

- **Fit Item: "The free space around it".** A third choice under *Fit it in*. The item grows as
  large as it can where it is and always stays the gap you set (*Gap to edges and items*) away from
  the screen's edges and from every other item.
  - Move, show or hide another item and the fitted item glides to its new space. *Glide to a new
    space over* sets how long, with a curve to choose; 0 changes at once.
  - Drag the fitted item to another part of the scene, or send it there with a key: it is left
    alone while it moves, then takes the free space around that place.
  - Backgrounds and frames are not in the way: anything the item's place lies inside is ignored.
  - **Items never in the way**: a list for items it should ignore, such as a camera meant to overlap
    the game.
- **Fit Item: "Measure by Arrange options"** (on as it comes). The items around it are measured the
  way the dock's Arrange options say: by what is drawn with *Use visible pixels*, with or without
  shadows by *Ignore shadows & outlines*. An item that is drawing nothing is not in the way. With
  shadows counted, the fitted item's own shadow keeps the gap as well, in every *Fit it in* choice.

## Good to know

- The gap is exact on the sides that limit the picture. A picture of another shape than the free
  space is centred in it, so its other two sides are further away.
- *The whole screen* and *A box I set* work as before: a picture of the box's own shape fills it.
- Match Item and Layout do not follow the Arrange options yet.

## Please check

1. On a scene with your game and camera side by side, set Fit Item to *The free space around it*
   with a gap of 30: the game should end 30 px from the screen's edges and 30 px from the camera.
2. Move or hide the camera: the game should glide to its new space.
