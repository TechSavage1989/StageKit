# StageKit 0.1.0-beta118

One hundred and eighteenth pre-release: Capture App in Use without the flicker, and the Capture
Front Window key works through it. It passes the automated tests on Linux; the Windows side has
not run in a real OBS yet.

## Fixed in Capture App in Use

- **The flicker right after a switch.** Your log showed it: the background capture of the app
  being switched to was shut down the moment the switch began, while the filter was still drawing
  from it, so its picture dropped out and came back. It now keeps running until the capture itself
  has the window.
- **The jump with every *Change with* but Morph.** The capture took the new window's size at once
  and the new picture came in afterwards. The size now glides for every style (a cut apart): with
  *Morph* both pictures are stretched over the gliding frame, with the others each keeps its own
  shape inside it.
- **The Capture Front Window key made the picture vanish.** It switched the filter off. It now
  hands the window to the filter, which stays on: the last picture stays until the new window is
  there and it comes in with your *Change with*.

## Changed

- **A window picked with Capture Front Window is remembered.** Its program is added to *Apps you
  switch to* and it is held until you go back. From then on the *Next Manual Capture* key (or the
  *next app switched to by hand* hotkey) is a toggle: one press returns to your apps, the next
  goes back to that window.

## Please check

1. Click between your apps with *Change with* on *A fade*, then on *Morph*. Is the flicker gone and
   the change of size smooth with both?
2. Press *Capture Front Window* on your browser. Does it come in smoothly and stay?
3. Press *Next Manual Capture* (On press: *Next capture or app*). Does it go back to your apps, and
   on the next press back to the browser?
