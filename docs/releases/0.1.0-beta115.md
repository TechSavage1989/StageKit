# StageKit 0.1.0-beta115

One hundred and fifteenth pre-release: one item can change into another inside a scene, Fade and
Zoom is a transition, and Blur can leave chosen captures alone. It passes the automated tests on
Linux; none of it has run in a real OBS window yet.

## New

- **Morph between items.** When one item is hidden and another is shown at the same moment, and
  both have *Morph (StageKit)* as their way of going and coming, the one moves, resizes and turns
  into the other. Choose *Morph (StageKit)* under *Change with* in Follow Active App, Program
  Trigger or Link Visibility, under *Show with* in Show in Scenes, or as an item's own *Show
  Transition* and *Hide Transition* in OBS.
  - Which two go together follows the Morph transition's rules: sources you have linked (in the
    Morph transition's settings under *Scene Transitions*, or in a Show in Scenes filter), then the
    largest item hidden with the largest item shown. Two captures in Follow Active App need no
    link.
  - It does not matter who hides and shows them: one filter, two filters, or two Stream Deck keys
    in one multi-action.
  - An item with nothing to change into fades by itself.
- **Show in Scenes: links for Morph.** With *Morph (StageKit)* chosen, *Link this source*, *With
  this source* and *Add this link* appear below, with the list of links made.
- **Fade and Zoom (StageKit)**, a new transition: an item grows to its size as it fades in and
  shrinks as it fades out. It is in every *Change with* and *Show with* list, in OBS's own item
  transitions, and under the plus in *Scene Transitions*. *Smallest size* sets how small it starts.
- **Blur: Never blur these.** A list under *And live captures on screen* of captures that tick box
  leaves alone, such as your camera.

## Good to know

- While one item changes into another, a locked item named *Morph (StageKit, in progress)* is in
  the scene for that moment and takes itself out again. You may see it flash by in the Sources list.
- It works in the scene that is on screen, for items straight in the scene. An item inside a group
  fades instead.

## Please check

1. In Follow Active App, set *Change with* to *Morph (StageKit)* and switch between two apps. Does
   the one capture move, resize and turn into the other?
2. In Show in Scenes, choose *Fade and Zoom (StageKit)* under *Show with* and hide and show a
   source. Does it grow in and shrink out?
3. In a Blur filter with *And live captures on screen* ticked, add your camera under *Never blur
   these* and switch the blur on. Does the camera stay clear?
