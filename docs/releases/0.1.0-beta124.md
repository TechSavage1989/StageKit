# StageKit 0.1.0-beta124

One hundred and twenty-fourth pre-release: the new control names you approved. Only the words
changed; every setting keeps its value.

## Changed

Labels in the filters' windows and the dock, shorter and in OBS's terms (*image* for picture,
*source* for item):

- **Fit to Space**: Ignored sources, Fit area, Free space around source, Gap, Move duration,
  Resize to fit, Align in the space, Sync position to scenes, Size tolerance.
- **Move Source / Move Scene Item**: Linked source, Destination, Duration.
- **Show/Hide Animation**: Direction, Show duration, Hide duration.
- **Blur**: Blur transition, Blur-in duration, Blur-out duration. **Zoom**: Zoom-in duration,
  Zoom-out duration.
- **Ambient Background**: Live capture, Live capture opacity, Bloom threshold.
- **Auto Frame**: Aspect ratio, Saved aspect ratios, Keep aspect ratio for.
- **Shadow, Glow & Outline**: Colour source (*The image itself*), Sizes in screen pixels.
- **Standby Picture**: Cover the capture now, Duration: image to capture, Duration: capture to
  image, Keep image's aspect ratio, Animate the size change.
- **Automatic Window Switcher**: Hide shows Standby Picture, Hide/show capture now.
- **Dock**: Move to, On drop.
