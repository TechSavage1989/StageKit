# StageKit 0.1.0-beta94

Ninety-fourth pre-release: two things the owner asked for. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## New

- **Fit to Space: *Resize it to fit*.** Ticked (as it comes), the filter works as before. Unticked,
  the item keeps the size it has and is only moved, to the middle of its space, so the room left
  over is balanced: the same on both sides, and the same above and below. When the space changes,
  for example a camera above a chat box is hidden, it moves to the new middle without changing
  size or shape. Resize it yourself in the preview and that is the size it keeps.
- **Dock, Copy tab: *Lock and unlock*.** Locks or unlocks items across scenes in one press.
  *Items*: the selected items' sources, the sources listed under *Sources to copy to*, or every
  source. *In*: all scenes, the scenes listed under *Scenes to copy to*, or this scene only. The
  copy tools leave locked copies alone, so *Unlock* frees them.

## Please check

1. The dock's *Update* button should offer this release.
2. Add Fit to Space to the scene for the chat box, choose *The free space around it*, untick
   *Resize it to fit*, and hide the camera: does the chat box move to the middle of the taller
   space at its own size?
3. On the Copy tab, do *Lock* and *Unlock* reach the scenes you expect?
