# StageKit 0.1.0-beta218

A source fitted to the whole screen can make room for the others when its image does not fill the screen.

## New

- **Make room if not filling.** A new tick box in Fit to Space and in each Scene Fit source's
  settings, for *Fit area* on *The whole screen* or *A box I set*. An image that fills the screen
  is fitted there as before, over the other sources. One that does not, such as a 4:3 standby
  image on a widescreen canvas, is fitted into the largest space clear of the other sources
  instead, so it sits beside a camera in the corner and no longer covers a sliver of it. When a
  widescreen image comes back it glides to the whole screen again.
- **The overlap rules for the whole screen.** With the box ticked, *Use fill percentage* with
  *Fill needed to overlap* and *Use edge gaps (pixels)* with its four sliders say what counts as
  filling the screen, and **Make room for** lists the sources room is made for (every other
  source when the list is empty).

## Changed

- **Clearer tooltip on Scene Fit's Overlap only when it fills.** It now says that each source's
  own rules are in that source's settings, and for which Fit areas they show.

## Good to know

- **You must tick the new box.** Nothing changes for existing filters until *Make room if not
  filling* is ticked for a source; it also needs *Overlap only when it fills* ticked (in a Scene
  Fit that is the one switch above the sources' settings). The Stream Deck *Fit to Space Overlap*
  key switches it as before: off, the image always takes the whole screen.
- **Backgrounds are never in the way.** Anything the middle of the screen lies inside, such as a
  background or a frame, is not made room for.
- **While ticked, the source moves like one in the free space**: with *Move duration* and easing,
  and a new image shape is taken about half a second after it settles.
- **Not yet seen on Windows.** It was tested headless on Linux only.
