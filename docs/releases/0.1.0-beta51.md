# StageKit 0.1.0-beta51

Fifty-first pre-release: the dock's *Send to* arrows can slide an item to the next edge. It passes the
automated tests on Linux; **it has not been run in OBS on Windows yet.**

## What's new

- **Slide to the next edge**, a tick box in the dock's *Send to* box. With it ticked, the arrows no
  longer send the item to a corner or a side of the screen. Instead each arrow slides it that way until
  it comes within the *Edge distance* of the next edge it meets:
  - the first press stops it short of the near side of the next item in its path, or of the screen's
    edge;
  - the next press goes through that edge to the item's far side, stopping the same distance inside
    it;
  - the press after that goes on to the next item, or to the screen's edge.
- The diagonal arrows slide across, then up or down. The centre button centres the item in the free
  space around it.
- *Use visible pixels* and *Ignore shadows* in the Arrange box decide what counts as an edge: the
  picture actually drawn, with or without its shadow, or the item's box.
- Nothing takes it past the screen's edge, and something thinner than the edge distance is passed in
  one press.
- Pressing again while it is still gliding carries on from where it is going, so quick presses step
  through edge after edge.
- An item chosen in *Bring along* travels with it and is never in its way.
- It glides for the *Travel time*. With *In every scene* it lands on the same spot of the screen in
  each of its scenes. A run of presses made while it is still moving is one step for Undo.
- Untick it and the arrows send the item to the nine screen positions as before. The tick box is
  remembered with your scene collection.

## Fixed

- **With *Every scene it is in*, an item inside a group could be moved twice**, the second time as if
  the group were the whole screen, so it landed in the wrong place in scenes where it sits in a group.
  This affected the dock's arrows and Stream Deck keys alike.
- **In Studio Mode, the dock's *Send to* with *In every scene* unticked moved the item in Program**
  instead of the Preview scene you were working on.
- Send To has a new destination, *A spot on the screen*, which the slides use.

## Installing on Windows

Close OBS and run `stagekit-0.1.0-windows-x64-Installer.exe`. It installs over an earlier version.

## Known limits

- Sliding is in the dock only; Stream Deck keys made with *Make 9 position keys* still go to the
  screen positions.
- The installer and the macOS package are not signed by a publisher.
- File names carry the base version, 0.1.0.
