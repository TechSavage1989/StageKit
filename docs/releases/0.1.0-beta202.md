# StageKit 0.1.0-beta202

Scene Fit costs next to nothing while nothing in its scene moves, on screen or off.

## Fixed

- **Scene Fit rests when nothing changes.** On screen, a Scene Fit whose sources are all in place, with nothing moved, shown, hidden, resized or redrawn in its scene, now works its sources out about twice a second instead of every frame. It still notices a change at once: a source shown or hidden, moved or resized by hand, a window switcher's change, another fit's new place or a widget that starts or stops drawing.
- **Scene Fit counts its sources less often.** With *All sources*, the scene's sources are counted again only when something in the scenes has changed (or every two seconds), and off screen only at its few checks a second.

## Good to know

- Measured headless on a synthetic collection with a Scene Fit in each layout scene: its share of StageKit's ticks went from about 80 to 110 µs a frame to about 30 on the scene on screen, most of what is left being the glances at widgets it did before as well. Not measured on Windows: if frames still drop, send the OBS log with its `timing, last 30 s:` lines.
