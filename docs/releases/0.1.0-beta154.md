# StageKit 0.1.0-beta154

One hundred and fifty-fourth pre-release: a show key can bring up its scene, the trial scenes have
new names, and their keys have images of their own. Tested on Linux only.

## Added

- **Show in Scenes: Switch to scene.** Choose a scene, and turning the switch on also changes OBS
  to that scene once its sources are shown (in Studio Mode, the preview). Nothing changes when the
  scene on screen already has one of the sources, and turning the switch off never changes scene.
  It works with any scene, so it fits any layout. *(none)* as it comes.
- **Stream Deck: images for Live, Chat Live and Standby.** Switch Scene keys for those scenes, and
  the show keys of the holding modes (starting, BRB, intermission, ending) and of the two columns,
  have images of their own, in every palette, marked on and off. An image you choose on a key still
  wins. Stream Deck plugin 0.17.4.0.

## Changed

- The trial scenes written by the merge scripts are called **Live** (was Full-Capture-Scene),
  **Chat Live** (was Component-Capture-Scene) and **Standby** (was Holding-Scene), and their
  switches have *Switch to scene* set: a capture's key goes to Live, a title's key to Standby, a
  column's key to Chat Live. A collection merged under the old names is refused: merge again from
  the file before it.
