# StageKit 0.1.0-beta212

Outlines get eleven new designs, five of them moving, in Shadow, Glow & Outline and Scene Shadow.

## New

- **Outline designs.** The outline of Shadow, Glow & Outline and of Scene Shadow has an *Outline
  design* list: Double line, Dashed, Dotted, Gradient (the outline colour into a second colour),
  Neon (a bright tube with a soft glow) and Inner line (just inside the image's edge).
- **Moving outlines.** Marching dashes, Travelling gradient, Pulse, Shimmer and Rainbow move at a
  *Speed* you set; 0 stands still and a negative number goes the other way.
- **Designs go with corners, shadows and Morph.** They combine with every *Outline corners*
  choice, the drop shadow is still thrown by the whole line, and a Morph between two scenes
  carries the design along.

## Good to know

- **Saved filters are unchanged.** Every outline stays *Solid* until you choose a design.
- **Pick corners for patterns that run round the border.** With *Outline corners* on Square,
  Rounded, Bevel, Notch or Scoop the dashes and colours follow the border. With *Follow the source*
  they are laid round the source's middle, and in Scene Shadow slanted across the whole scene.
  *Inner line* needs corners chosen too.
- **Not yet seen on Windows.** The new designs have only run on OpenGL here. If one looks wrong on
  Windows, switch back to *Solid*, which is drawn as before, and tell us which design it was.
