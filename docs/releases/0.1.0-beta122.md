# StageKit 0.1.0-beta122

One hundred and twenty-second pre-release: Fit to Space's overlap rule gets a switch of its own, a
clearer name and a Stream Deck key, and Standby Picture gets a Morph transition. It passes the automated tests on Linux; the Stream Deck key
has not run in Stream Deck here.

## Changed

- **Fit to Space: "Avoid them under a fill of" is now two controls.** *Overlap only when it
  fills*, a tick box, switches the rule on and off. *Fill needed to overlap* is how much of its
  space the fitted item must fill to go under the *Items never in the way*; under that, it is
  fitted beside them. What you set in beta121 is kept and reads as switched on.

- **Standby Picture shown over a running capture now changes from the live picture.** The
  capture keeps running under the picture until the picture is fully in, and the change uses the
  filter's own *Transition*, *To the capture* and *Back to the picture* times. In the tests here
  it fades in and out; if it still cuts on your machine, the OBS log now has a line
  `Standby Picture '...': shown over the capture, over ... ms` that says what it was set to do.

## New

- **Standby Picture: Morph.** A new *Transition*: the capture and the picture are both stretched
  over the frame as it glides from one shape to the other, cross-fading. *Zoom and fade* is now
  listed as *Fade and zoom*, as everywhere else.

- **Stream Deck key: Fit to Space Overlap** (Stream Deck plugin 0.10.0.0). Choose the scene's Fit
  to Space filter; the key switches *Overlap only when it fills* (toggle, on or off) and shows
  which way it is, also when you change it in OBS.
