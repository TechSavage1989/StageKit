# StageKit 0.1.0-beta120

One hundred and twentieth pre-release: the cause of the choppiness, a transition and a delay for
the Ambient Background when the capture changes, the bloom from your old collection, and a
smoother change between manual and automatic. It passes the automated tests on Linux; the Windows
side has not run in a real OBS here.

## Fixed

- **Choppiness during transitions and moves.** Your logs showed what it was: Auto Capture had
  taken your 3D modelling program for a game, and OBS's Game Capture then tried to hook it about
  five times a second, without end (3,404 tries in one twelve-minute session). Each try stalls OBS
  for about a fiftieth of a second, so one frame in fourteen ran late, all the time, in every
  scene. Auto Capture now gives up: a program that gives the capture no picture for ten seconds is
  put on *Never capture* by itself, and programs listed in an Automatic Window Switcher are never
  taken at all.
- **Manual to automatic and back: the next window was there at a stroke, then resized.** The
  picture now changes together with its size, as it does between your automatic windows.

## New in Ambient Background

- **Capture transition, Capture delay, Duration, easing.** When the capture blended in changes,
  the background as it was stays for the delay and then gives way: a fade (as it comes), a cut, or
  a slide to any side. Set the delay to the length of the capture's own transition to have the
  background change after the picture has.
- **Bloom style: Whole picture, amplified.** The bloom of your old collection (the shader filter's
  bloom): the picture's own light added back onto all of it, so everything brightens and colours
  run into white. 100 % is that shader's strength of 1; your old scenes used 4 to 10, so try 400 %
  to 1000 %, with a small *Bloom size*. The style there was before is *Bright parts shine*.

## Good to know

- The first time, Auto Capture needs its ten seconds to give up on the program. You can also do
  it at once: in the Auto Capture filter, pick the program and press *Never capture it*.
- beta119's change to the window switcher (no capture started at a switch) stays: it was worth
  doing, but it was not what made the frames late.

4. Toggle between manual and automatic. Does the picture change together with its size now?
