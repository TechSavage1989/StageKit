# StageKit 0.1.0-beta156

One hundred and fifty-sixth pre-release: a fix for sources that popped in at the end of a Morph
instead of fading in. Tested on Linux only.

The work to bring the frame time down, and a new scene collection built with the scene filters,
come in the next beta (beta157).

## Fixed

- **A source shown with Morph and nothing to change from now fades in.** When one source is hidden
  and two are shown at the same moment (the DSLR hidden, a capture and the square camera shown), the
  hidden one morphs into one of them. The other drew nothing for the whole change and then appeared
  at once at its end, after Ambient Background had already changed to it. It now fades in during
  the change (or zooms or slides, as Morph's *Other sources* says). Changing from one capture to
  another was not affected: there, one source goes and one comes, and they always morph.
- **A source hidden with Fade and Zoom** stayed whole until the end and then vanished. It now fades
  and shrinks on the way out.

Ambient Background was not at fault: it followed the new capture on time, but the capture itself
was not drawn. Its *Capture delay* can go back to 0.

## Not yet tried on Windows

- Both fixes follow OBS's own code for item show and hide transitions and are tested headless with
  OBS 30; they have not run in OBS 32 on Windows.
