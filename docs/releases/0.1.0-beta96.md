# StageKit 0.1.0-beta96

Ninety-sixth pre-release: fixes to Fit to Space from the owner's testing, and a stronger blur. It
passes the automated tests on Linux.

## Fixed

- **With *Resize it to fit* unticked, an item could be pushed off the screen.** When a neighbour
  grew until the free space was narrower than the item, the item was still put in the middle of
  that space, which could lie past the screen's edge. It now always stays the gap inside the
  screen's edges; a neighbour with its own Fit to Space then makes room for it.
- **Fit to Space added to a group did strange things.** On a group the filter took the group itself
  for the screen. It now does nothing there and says so at the top of its settings. Add it to the
  **scene** (right-click the scene, *Filters*) and choose the group as its *Item*.

## Changed

- **Blur: *Strength* goes up to 1000** (it stopped at 250). A Gaussian or box blur set above 250 is
  drawn the way *Dual Kawase* is, which stays smooth at that strength. Filters set to 250 or less
  look exactly as before.
