# StageKit 0.1.0-beta142

One hundred and forty-second pre-release: the transitions between a hidden source and its standby
image work when the source has a shadow or shaped corners. Tested on Linux only.

## Fixed

- **Standby Image, *Show when source is hidden*: most transitions switched in place.** With a
  Shadow, Glow & Outline or a Corners & Edges after Standby Image on the source, the standby
  image appeared at once when the source was hidden, and when it was shown the standby image
  stayed, or flashed, and then cut to the source. OBS keeps one image per filter per frame, so
  while the source and its standby image were both on screen they both showed the same image.
  Those two filters now draw their input afresh each time, and the source and the image are
  apart all the way through a slide, a fade or any other transition.
  - **Filter order matters.** After Standby Image, only StageKit's Shadow, Glow & Outline and
    Corners & Edges are safe. Any other filter (OBS's own colour correction or crop, StageKit's
    Blur or Zoom, another plugin's) should come before Standby Image in the list, or the change
    will cut as before.

## Changed

- The first transition list in the Standby Image filter is now called **No-signal transition**,
  to tell it from *Show/Hide transition*. It is used while the source is shown but has nothing
  to show; *Show/Hide transition* is used when the source is hidden or shown in a scene.
