# StageKit 0.1.0-beta183

Hiding a capture with its key and showing it again no longer shows it where it was meant to stay
hidden.

## Fixed

- **Hide or show the capture** (Auto Capture and Automatic Window Switcher, the hotkey under the
  capture, the filter's *Hide/show capture now* button and the Stream Deck *Manual Window /
  Source* key set to *Hide or show the capture*). Without a Standby Image to cover the capture,
  the show used to show the capture in every scene, including scenes where it was hidden on
  purpose: a camera with a leftover Auto Capture came up full-screen over the capture a Link
  Visibility had on, and over modes that were not on. Now the hide remembers where it hid the
  capture, and the show brings it back there only, and not where a mode that is not on holds it
  hidden. A press with nothing remembered and the capture shown nowhere does nothing. What is
  remembered is kept when OBS restarts, and the key reads *hidden* until the capture is shown
  again. With a Standby Image the hide covers the capture as before.
