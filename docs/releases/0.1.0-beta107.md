# StageKit 0.1.0-beta107

One hundred and seventh pre-release: Fit to Space reacts to a widget in a fraction of a second and
no longer stalls the picture to do it. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## Fixed

- **Choppy preview with Fit to Space filters in the scene.** To notice a widget that starts or
  stops drawing, every filter looked properly at one item a second, and each look held the picture
  up for a few milliseconds (your log showed single steps of 8 to 45 ms). The filters now glance
  at the items instead: a very small copy, read back two frames later, which costs next to
  nothing. A proper look happens only when a glance shows that something changed.
- **Slow to make room for a widget that appears or disappears.** With one item looked at per
  second, a widget could go unnoticed for as many seconds as the scene has items. It is now
  noticed within a few frames; items that changed lately are glanced at most often.
