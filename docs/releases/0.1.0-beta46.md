# StageKit 0.1.0-beta46

Forty-sixth pre-release: guide lines in the preview. It passes the automated tests on Linux; **it has
not been run in OBS on Windows yet, and it has never been seen over OBS's real preview.**

## What's new

- **Guides in the preview** (off until you switch it on). Tick it in the dock's *Arrange* box or in the
  preview's right-click *StageKit* menu. Then:
  - while you drag an item in the preview, a line appears across the canvas where one of its edges or
    its centre comes within reach of the screen's edge or centre, or of another item's. It is the line
    *Snap on release* would move it to;
  - when you use a line-up tool (*Left*, *Centre*, *Right*, *Top*, *Middle*, *Bottom*), the line the
    items were put on shows for a moment.
- The lines are on your screen only. They are never in the stream, a recording or the program output.

## How it works, and what to check

OBS gives plugins no way to draw in its preview. The lines are drawn in a see-through window that sits
exactly over the preview, lets every click through, and only exists while a line is showing. Where the
canvas sits inside the preview is worked out the same way OBS does it for **Scale to Window**; with a
fixed preview zoom the lines will be in the wrong place.

Because this could not be tried in OBS here, please look for two things: whether the lines sit exactly
on the edges they refer to, and whether the preview still behaves normally while a line is showing.
If either is off, untick *Guides in the preview* and tell me what you saw.

Measuring what is drawn in each item happens once, when a drag starts.

## Installing on Windows

Close OBS and run `stagekit-0.1.0-windows-x64-Installer.exe`. It installs over an earlier version.

## Known limits

- Guides assume the preview is set to *Scale to Window*.
- The installer and the macOS package are not signed by a publisher.
- File names carry the base version, 0.1.0.
