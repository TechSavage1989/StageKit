# StageKit 0.1.0-beta121

One hundred and twenty-first pre-release: blur with Bloom on, the Bloom slider, a fill rule for
Fit to Space's *Items never in the way*, and hiding a capture to its Standby Picture. It passes
the automated tests on Linux; the Windows side has not run in a real OBS here.

## Fixed

- **Ambient Background: the blur with Bloom on.** The bloom's light was taken from the picture
  before the blur and spread only by *Bloom size*, so a small size laid a sharper picture back
  over the blurred one. The light now always spreads at least as far as *Blur*.
- **Ambient Background: the Bloom slider could not be dragged.** Moving it showed and hid the
  other bloom controls, which made OBS rebuild the window at every step. The bloom controls now
  stay in place.

## New

- **Fit to Space: Avoid them under a fill of.** For the *Items never in the way*. At 0 (as it
  comes) nothing changes. Above 0, those items are only overlapped while the fitted picture fills
  at least that much of its space. At 95 %, a window that fills the screen stays under the camera;
  a window of another shape, which would leave empty bars, is moved and resized to sit beside the
  camera instead, with the usual glide.
- **Hiding a capture brings in its Standby Picture.** With a Standby Picture filter on the same
  capture, the Automatic Window Switcher's *Hide or show it now*, its hotkey and the Stream Deck
  *Hide or show the capture* key now change to the Standby Picture, the way that filter is set to
  change, and back. The item stays in place, so nothing around it moves. Untick *Hide to the
  Standby Picture* in the switcher to hide the item as before.
- **Standby Picture: Show or hide the picture now.** The same on any source, as a button in the
  filter and a hotkey on the source.
