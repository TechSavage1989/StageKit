# StageKit 0.1.0-beta111

One hundred and eleventh pre-release: transitions with their own settings in Show in Scenes, and
linked sources in the Morph transition. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## New

- **Show in Scenes: *Show with* and *Hide with*.** Showing and hiding each have their own
  transition and duration. Hiding is *The same as showing* until you choose otherwise, so an item
  can slide down into view and slide up out of it.
- **Show in Scenes: a transition's own settings, in the filter.** Choose Slide, Swipe, Fade to
  Color or Luma Wipe and its own settings appear under it: the direction, the colour and so on.
- **Show in Scenes: each source its own.** With sources on the list, *Settings for* switches the
  controls between *All sources (the usual)* and one source. A source can show and hide another
  way than the rest: one slides, another fades.
- **Morph: *Linked sources*.** Two different sources that are to count as the same thing: your
  camera in one scene and another camera in the next. Choose the two under *Link this source* and
  *With this source*, press *Add this link*, and the one moves, resizes and changes into the
  other. Add as many links as you like; each has its own remove button.

## Fixed

- **Fit to Space could miss a widget in a scene made after another was deleted.** What it had seen
  of the deleted scene's items was kept and taken for the new scene's. It is now let go when a
  filter joins or leaves a scene.

## Please check

1. In a Show in Scenes filter, choose *Slide* under *Show with* and set its direction; set another
   direction under *Hide with*. Does the item come and go the way you set?
2. Choose one source under *Settings for* and give it a fade. Does it fade while the others slide?
3. In Morph's properties, link your two cameras and switch between their scenes. Does the one
   turn into the other?
