# StageKit 0.1.0-beta208

Renaming a source right after removing a Fit to Space or Scene Fit no longer risks a crash.

## Fixed

- **Renaming a source just after removing a fit filter is safe.** Fit to Space, Scene Fit, Scene
  Modes and Scene Transitions could read a filter OBS was still taking apart when a source was
  renamed at that moment; they now let go of such a filter cleanly.
