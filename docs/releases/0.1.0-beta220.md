# StageKit 0.1.0-beta220

Position X, Position Y and Rotation for Ambient Background and for the patterns and colours in an outline.

## New

- **Pose the Ambient Background.** *Position X*, *Position Y* and *Rotation* in its Image group
  move and turn the background, whatever it is made from. An image or source still covers the whole
  screen and keeps drifting the way you set. A pattern's own layout is moved and turned: Circuit
  traces can come in from a side, the centre of Rays can sit elsewhere, Sun grid can tilt.
- **Pose the pattern in an outline.** In Shadow, Glow & Outline and in Scene Shadow, an outline
  filled with a pattern has *Position X*, *Position Y* and *Rotation* for the pattern inside the
  line. The line itself stays where it is.
- **Turn a gradient or slide the dashes.** *Rotation* also shows for Gradient, Travelling, Shimmer
  and Rainbow, where it sets where round the border the colours begin, and for Dashed and
  Marching, where it slides the dashes along the line.

## Good to know

- **Nothing changes until you move them.** All three are 0 as they come, and every saved look is
  drawn exactly as before.
- **Only run on OpenGL.** The shaders behind this have been tried on Linux (OpenGL) only, not on
  Windows (Direct3D). If a background or an outline pattern goes missing after this update, say so.
- Solid, Double, Dotted, Neon, Inner and Pulse outlines have nothing to pose and show none of the
  three controls.
