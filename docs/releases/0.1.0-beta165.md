# StageKit 0.1.0-beta165

Scene Blur can blur each source by itself, and a mosaic can keep to whole cells at a source's edge.

## Added

- **Scene Blur: Blur area.** A new choice on Scene Blur:
  - *The scene as drawn* (as it comes, and what every Scene Blur you already have keeps doing):
    the finished scene is blurred and the blur is kept to the chosen sources. Along a source's
    edge the blur takes in what is around it, and a mosaic's cells are laid out over the whole
    screen.
  - *Each source by itself*: every chosen source is blurred from its own image only, as a Blur
    filter on that source would blur it. The blur goes cleanly to the source's edges, a mosaic's
    cells are laid out over that source, and with the same settings it looks the same as the Blur
    filter on the source.
- **Mosaic edge: Whole cells.** For Pixelate, on the Blur filter and on Scene Blur with *Each
  source by itself*: a cell that the source's edge would cut is left out, so the blurred source's
  outline is made of whole cells (hexagons, circles and so on). The image never grows past its own
  edge, so cells are only ever left out. *Cut at the edge* is how it was before and stays the
  setting as it comes.
- **Shadows, glows and outlines take the blurred shape.** A Shadow, Glow & Outline or Corners &
  Edges placed after a Blur filter with whole cells follows the cells. A Scene Shadow on a scene
  whose Scene Blur blurs each source by itself casts its shadow, glow and outline from the blurred
  images, whole cells included, and the shadows stay sharp.

## Good to know

- *Each source by itself* draws the scene's sources once more inside the chosen sources' boxes;
  with many chosen sources it costs more than blurring the scene as a whole.
- Where a source is cropped in the scene, *Each source by itself* blurs only the part that shows;
  a Blur filter on the source blurs the whole source before it is cropped, so the two differ a
  little along a crop.
- The new shader code has only been run on OpenGL. If Windows logs an error about
  `stagekit-looks.effect`, please send the log.
