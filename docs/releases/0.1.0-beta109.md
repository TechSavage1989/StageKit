# StageKit 0.1.0-beta109

One hundred and ninth pre-release: Fit to Space's quick check on widgets costs the graphics card
far less. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## Fixed

- **Frame time went up on beta107.** To notice a widget coming and going, the Fit to Space filters
  drew one item of the scene small fifteen times a second, the game capture and the background
  with all their filters among them. Now only items that can come and go are checked often: browser
  widgets, text, groups and nested scenes, and anything that has once been seen to change, about
  twice a second each. Everything else is checked about every ten seconds, one item at a time.
- A widget is still made room for in about half a second (it was a tenth on beta107, and several
  seconds before that).

## Please check

1. In OBS, open View, Stats and look at *Average time to render frame* with a fitted scene on
   screen. Is it back to what it was before beta107?
2. Does the chat box still move promptly when the music widget appears and disappears?
