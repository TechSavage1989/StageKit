# StageKit 0.1.0-beta214

Ambient Background can draw its own animated backgrounds: ten patterns in your colours, with no image needed.

## New

- **Animated patterns for Ambient Background.** Under *Background from*, choose *A pattern*, then
  one of ten: Aurora, Plasma, Lava lamp, Waves, Bokeh, Starfield, Gradient flow, Nebula, Grid
  pulse and Rays.
- **Your colours, speed, size and detail.** Three palette colours, *Speed* (0 holds it still, under
  0 runs it backwards), *Size* and *Detail*. At 100 % speed a pattern comes back to where it began
  every four minutes, with no jump.
- **Everything else still applies.** A pattern takes the image's place: the blur, the mosaic (a
  hexagon mosaic over Plasma, say), bloom, Darkest level, the texture overlay and a live capture
  blended in all act on it.

## Good to know

- The blur softens a pattern a great deal. Turn *Blur* down to see a pattern's shapes clearly.
- Drift, zoom and mirroring are for an image; they are hidden while a pattern is chosen.
- Existing backgrounds are unchanged, and a new one still starts from the standby image.
- The patterns' shaders have only run on OpenGL here and have not been seen on Windows. If they
  fail to load on Windows, the background shows the standby image (or its image file) instead and
  OBS's log has the line *Ambient Background: the patterns are not available*.
- *Colours from capture* is not in this version.
