# StageKit 0.1.0-beta82

Eighty-second pre-release: the StageKit dock, reorganised. Everything in it works as before; nothing
was removed. It passes the automated tests on Linux; **it has not been seen in OBS on Windows yet.**

## Changed

- **The dock is four tabs instead of one long column.** *Scene* and *Item* stay at the top.
  - **Place**: the nine positions, *Bring along*, edge distance, travel time and motion, the slide
    and every-scene options, and the buttons that make Stream Deck keys.
  - **Arrange**: what is selected, *Align to*, the line-up, space-out, snap and size buttons, the
    order buttons, and the options in two short groups: *How the tools measure* and *When I let go
    of an item*.
  - **Copy**: the two copy-transform buttons with their lists of scenes and sources.
  - **Effects**: the buttons that add a StageKit filter to the item.
- The tallest tab is about half the height the dock used to be, so it rarely needs to scroll.
- The arrange and order buttons have small drawings of what they do.
- The position arrows are larger; the motion curve and its direction share one row.
- What the last press did is shown under the tabs, whichever one is open.
