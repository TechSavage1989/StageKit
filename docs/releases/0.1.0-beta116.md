# StageKit 0.1.0-beta116

One hundred and sixteenth pre-release: one Window Capture can follow several apps. It passes the
automated tests on Linux; the Windows side has not run in a real OBS yet.

## New

- **Capture App in Use**, a filter for a Window Capture. List the apps the capture is for, and the
  filter aims that one capture at whichever of them you are using. A scene then needs one capture,
  one set of filters and one Fit to Space, instead of a capture for each app.
  - It follows the same rules as the scene filter it stands in for: an app that opens is captured;
    clicking into one of the apps captures it (*Follow the app in use*); clicking into anything
    else changes nothing; when the app being captured closes, the one you used before it comes
    back.
  - Apps are chosen from the ones that are open, by program, or by a window's title for two
    programs with the same file name.
  - The change between two apps is drawn by the filter: the last picture stays until the capture
    has the new window, and the new one comes in over it. *Change with* offers a fade (as it
    comes), a cut, a fade and zoom, or a slide in from any side, with *Lasting* and easing.
  - A Standby Picture on the capture does not stand in between two apps; it does once none of them
    is open.
  - To hold a window that is not on the list, use the Stream Deck *Capture Front Window* key: it
    switches the filter off. Switch the filter on again to hand the capture back to your apps.
- **Stream Deck plugin 0.7.0.0**: a *Capture App in Use* key that switches the filter on and off.

## Changed

- **Follow Active App is now called Show Source in Use**, in OBS's filter list, the README and on
  its Stream Deck key. Nothing else about it changed: filters already in your scenes keep working
  and keep the name you gave them, and keys already on your deck keep working.

## Good to know

- Capture App in Use puts itself first in the capture's filter list when you add it. Leave it
  there, before Standby Picture, Auto Frame and the rest.
- How a real Window Capture behaves in the moment it is aimed at another window could not be tried
  here. If the change is not smooth (the old window flashes back, or there is a gap), send OBS's
  log from that session.

## Please check

1. Add *Capture App in Use (StageKit)* to your *Window Capture*, list your apps,
   and click from one to another. Does the capture follow, and is the change smooth?
2. Close the app that is showing. Does the one you used before it come back?
3. With none of them open, does the Standby Picture show?
4. Press your *Capture Front Window* key on another window, then switch the filter on again. Does
   it hold the window, and then go back to your apps?
