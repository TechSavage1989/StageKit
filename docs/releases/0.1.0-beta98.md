# StageKit 0.1.0-beta98

Ninety-eighth pre-release: *Use visible pixels* for the fitted item itself, and a stronger Ambient
Background blur. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## Changed

- **Fit to Space: *Use visible pixels* now counts for the item being fitted too.** Until now it
  only decided how the items around it were measured; the fitted item went by its own box. A group,
  a widget or a nested scene whose box is larger than what it shows is now fitted, centred and kept
  clear by what shows. The gap is measured to the picture you can see, and the empty part of the
  box may reach into the gap. Untick it to go by boxes, as before.
- **Ambient Background: *Blur* goes up to 1000** (it stopped at 250), like the Blur filter since
  beta96.

## Good to know

- The filter looks at what the item shows when it first meets it, shortly after its source changes
  size, and every few seconds while it sits still. A chat box whose background is see-through and
  whose text comes and goes may therefore shift a little as its text changes; a panel with a solid
  background, like yours, will not. Say so if you see it move by itself.
