# StageKit 0.1.0-beta77

Seventy-seventh pre-release: the causes of the dropped frames that the timing log showed. It passes
the automated tests on Linux; **it has not been run in OBS on Windows yet.**

## Fixed

- **Auto Capture and Active App held frames up, all the time.** Four times a second they looked
  through every open window, on the same thread OBS draws with. Usually that takes a moment; in the
  log it took 20 to 70 ms again and again, and up to a third of a second. A separate thread now
  keeps the list of windows, and the filters only read it.
- **Every camera move began with a pause.** A Send To key that moves the camera in every scene
  measured the camera (and what it brings along) in each scene before moving: 40 to 70 ms. It now
  looks at each source once per press. The move itself also does less work on every frame.
- **The game capture stopped and started again at every scene switch.** OBS stops a capture a moment
  after you leave the last scene that shows it and hooks it again when you return. Auto Capture has
  a new tick box, **Keep capturing in other scenes** (on as it comes), which keeps it running.

## Not StageKit

- Your capture card source reports *Video configuration failed* each time a scene with it is shown,
  because the device is not connected. That is OBS trying to open it, and it may cost a moment at
  those scene switches until the card is plugged in.

2. Send the log of that session again, whatever the result.
