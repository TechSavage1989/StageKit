# StageKit 0.1.0-beta131

One hundred and thirty-first pre-release: the window capture comes back with its scene, and the
overlap rule's edge amounts are sliders with clearer names. It passes the automated tests on
Linux; the Windows side has not run in a real OBS here.

## Fixed

- **The window capture was gone after coming back to its scene.** With an Automatic Window
  Switcher, going to a scene without the capture and back left it empty until another of the
  listed windows was switched to. While the capture was in no scene on show, the switcher stopped
  every background capture, the one it was showing too, and did not take it up again on the way
  back. It now keeps the shown window captured while its scene is away (the other windows'
  background captures still stop, and start again on the way back), and takes the picture up
  again by itself should it ever be lost.

## Changed

- **Overlap by edge gaps is clearer.** In Fit to Space, *Use edge distances* is now **Use edge gaps
  (pixels)**, and its four amounts are sliders with a number box, like the percentage: **Top gap
  up to**, **Bottom gap up to**, **Left gap up to**, **Right gap up to**. Each is the widest empty
  gap allowed between the fitted window and that edge of its space. The window goes under the
  camera only when all four gaps are within their sliders; 0 means the window must reach that
  edge. Example: a window that fills the width and leaves 20 px above and below overlaps the
  camera with top and bottom at 30. A narrower window that leaves 300 px at the sides goes beside
  the camera, unless left and right are set to 300 or more. Your settings are kept.

## Added

- **The icon pack's editable SVGs** are in the repository, all six palettes, under
  `streamdeck/svg/pack/`.
