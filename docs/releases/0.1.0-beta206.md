# StageKit 0.1.0-beta206

Scene Modes can put sources in a place of each mode's own, and shows one mode's settings at a time.

## New

- **Each mode can place sources where you saved them.** Under a mode, list sources in *Placed in this mode*, arrange them by hand with the mode on and press *Save their places now*: when the mode comes on they glide to that place, size, crop and bounding box. A widget shown in two modes can sit under the chat in one and match the handheld windows in the other.

## Changed

- **Scene Modes shows one mode's settings at a time.** Choose the mode under *Settings for*; the settings for all modes, below them, no longer need a long scroll.

## Good to know

- **Not yet seen on Windows.** A Fit to Space or Scene Fit on a placed source takes the new place but may still adjust it by its own rules.
