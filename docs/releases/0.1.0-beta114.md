# StageKit 0.1.0-beta114

One hundred and fourteenth pre-release: a shadow, glow or outline keeps its thickness while its
item is hidden, so it no longer comes in thin and snaps part-way. It passes the automated tests on
Linux.

## Fixed

- **A shadow or outline came in thinner and snapped to its thickness part-way through.** With
  *Sizes as they look on screen* ticked, Shadow, Glow & Outline and Corners & Edges work their sizes out from
  how big the item is drawn in the scene on screen, and they passed over an item that was hidden.
  While hidden, the sizes went by a copy of the source in some other scene, or by no scaling at
  all, and were put right only a moment after the item was shown: about a third of a second, which
  is most of a slide. A hidden item now counts at the size it will be shown at, so the effect is
  already the right thickness when the item comes in, and it stays so while the item goes out.
- Because the effect's thickness is part of the item's size, that change part-way also made items
  fitted round it with Fit to Space move a second time. With the size steady from the start they
  have one thing to answer.

## Please check

1. Hide and show an item that has a shadow or an outline, with a slide set in Show in Scenes. Is
   the effect the same thickness all the way in and out?
2. Do the items fitted round it now move once, smoothly?
