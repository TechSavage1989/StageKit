# StageKit 0.1.0-beta187

Ambient Background can be kept from ever going black: a Darkest level, lifted toward the background image or a colour.

## New

- **Darkest level for Ambient Background.** In the Look group: the background never gets darker
  than this, even while the capture blended in is dark. Parts below the level are lifted
  smoothly; parts well above it are left as they are. 0 % (as it comes) is off, so your
  backgrounds look as they did.
- **Lift toward.** *The background image*: dark parts show the image (or source) the background
  is made from, blurred and coloured as the background is, so a dark capture fades into the usual
  background. *A colour*: dark parts take on the **Lift colour** (a mid grey as it comes), as bright
  as the level.

## Good to know

- A capture that is black all over for two seconds already counted as no capture, and the usual
  image showed; the Darkest level is for a capture that is dark but not empty.
- With nothing under *Background from*, the colour is used.
- Costs one extra full-screen pass while on, plus one blurred copy of the image while a capture is
  in and the fill is the image. Not yet tried on Windows.
