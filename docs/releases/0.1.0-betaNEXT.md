# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

One fix to Morph as a show and hide transition.

## Fixed

- **OBS could keep sources after closing or changing scene collection when Morph was used for
  showing and hiding sources.** After a source was shown or hidden with Morph, StageKit kept hold
  of its scene for up to 20 seconds, longer than the change itself. Changing scene collection or
  closing OBS in that time could leave the scene and its sources behind: OBS's "Not all sources
  were cleared" message (Source Cleanup Error) at a change of collection, and errors in the log
  as OBS closed. StageKit now holds nothing of a scene once a change is drawn, and lets go of
  every change under way as soon as the scene collection starts changing.

## Good to know

- A Morph between two sources that is under way when the scene collection changes ends there and
  then; the new collection starts without it.
