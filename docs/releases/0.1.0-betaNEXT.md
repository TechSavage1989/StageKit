# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Align in the space always decides where a fitted source sits, for any layout, and is chosen on a grid like OBS's own.

## New

- **An alignment grid in the dock.** Beside *Fit to space* on the Place tab, nine small squares, one checked, as in OBS's Edit Transform. They show the selected source's *Align in the space* and set it on its Fit to Space or Scene Fit entry when pressed; for a source with no fit filter they are how the dock's *Fit to space* places it.

## Changed

- **Align in the space always decides.** In Fit to Space, in Scene Fit and for the dock's *Fit to space*, a source lies in its space where its *Align in the space* puts it, kept at its size or resized, whatever lies beside it. A source that keeps its size is no longer pushed against the far side when something beside it makes room, nor packed against a resized image above or below it. The rule is the same for every layout.
- **Align in the space lists its nine places as a grid.** The list in the filter windows now goes row by row from the top left, each place with an arrow (↖ ↑ ↗, ← • →, ↙ ↓ ↘), the order of OBS's own bounding box alignment. OBS's filter window cannot show a grid of buttons. Saved settings are unchanged.

## Fixed

- **Fits beside each other come to rest.** A resized source goes by where a source that keeps its size belongs, not by where its alignment puts it in the room left, so the two settle in one move and stay.

## Good to know

- Not seen on Windows.
- Switching a fit on, or updating, can move a source that keeps its size to where its *Align in the space* puts it in its space: a chat box set to *The middle* under a camera now sits in the middle of the room under it, not packed against the camera. Set it to *The top* to keep it the gap under the camera's image.
- A column that keeps its size beside a resized capture (a camera and chat box beside a game) and is set to *The middle* now lies in the middle of the room the capture leaves, so it moves when the capture changes shape. To keep it against the screen's edge, set its *Align in the space* to that side (*The right*, say).
