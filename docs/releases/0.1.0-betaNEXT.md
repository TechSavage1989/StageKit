# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

The Neon outline is a real neon tube now: a bright line in your colour with a soft halo of light that spreads out past it.

## Changed

- **Neon outline glows outwards.** The *Neon* outline design used to squeeze its glow inside the
  line's own width and turned the line white. Now the whole line is the tube, in the outline
  colour and brighter along its middle (never plain white unless the colour is white), and a soft
  halo of the same colour spreads out past the line and fades away. The halo is light: it adds to
  what is behind it instead of covering it, so it looks lit on a dark background. In both
  Shadow, Glow & Outline and Scene Shadow, with every kind of *Outline corners*, round text, and
  through a Morph. The drop shadow is still thrown by the line itself.
- **Glow size is how far the halo reaches.** In per cent of the outline's width, past the line:
  150 % as it comes, up to 400 %. A value you had set before (10 to 90) gives a small halo;
  raise it for more. In Shadow, Glow & Outline the halo takes a little more room round the source;
  the image stays where it is.
- **Second colour shows for Neon.** It is only used when the outline colour is very dark: then
  the halo takes the *Second colour* if that is brighter.

## Good to know

- Pick a bright outline colour for Neon. A black or very dark line has no light to give: it shows
  as a dark line with a dim rim and no halo, unless the *Second colour* is brighter.
- The other outline designs are unchanged, pixel for pixel.
- The new halo has only been run on OpenGL here. If Neon looks wrong on Windows, or an outline
  stops showing at all, please send OBS's log.
