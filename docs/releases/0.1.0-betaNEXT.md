# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Tidier filter windows: controls in groups and in one order, controls that do nothing hidden, and one line of help at the end of each window. Your settings and saved filters are unchanged.

## Changed

- **One line of help in every filter window.** The paragraph at the bottom of a filter's window is
  now one line saying where the filter goes and what switching it on does. The six scene filters
  that had none have one too. The detail is in the tooltips and the guide.
- **Scene filters in one order.** Every scene filter's window runs: what it applies to, *Sources
  covered*, its lists, the look, the timing, the transition, then any button. Scene Corners, Scene
  Shadow and Scene Blur have the same *Applied to* choice, with *Each source by itself* first.
  *Ignored sources* hides in Scene Transitions and Scene Shadow when *Chosen sources* is picked, as
  in the others. The warning on a group is one wrapped sentence in every scene filter.
- **Scene Standby in two groups.** *When a source has no image* (its tick box switches the whole
  group) and *When a source is hidden*.
- **Fit to Space in numbered groups.** *1. Where*, *2. Size*, *3. Sharing the space*,
  *4. Measuring*, *5. Movement*, then *Sync position to scenes*; the same for each source in Scene
  Fit. *Use visible pixels* shows for every *Fit area*, where it always counted. *Make room if not
  filling*'s tooltip says why the filling rules are hidden while it is unticked.
- **Scene Modes adds a mode in one row.** A template, a name and *Add mode*. *Leave to* shows only
  when *Leave when shown* has a source.
- **Captures regrouped.** Auto Capture: *Games*, *When there is no game*, and the hiding controls in
  the Window Switcher's *Show/Hide transition* group, whose transition shows only while a standby
  option is ticked; *Keep the capture ready*. Automatic Window Switcher: *Windows*, *Switching*,
  *Manual windows*, then *Show/Hide transition*, with tooltips on its buttons; *When a window is
  picked* and *Clear picked windows at start*. Standby Image: *Show/Hide transition* shows only with
  *Show when source is hidden*, and *Cover the source now* is last.
- **Transition lists in one order.** The lists StageKit draws itself (Standby Image, Scene Standby,
  Auto Capture, the Window Switcher) read *Fade*, *Morph*, *Fade and zoom*, *Cut*, then the slides
  and wipes, each named for the way the image moves. Saved choices are kept.
- **Blur, Shadow and Auto Frame.** Blur's three lists of other sources are one group, *Blur other
  sources too*, and *Never blur these* shows only with live captures ticked; *Include in Blur All*
  is in *Switching*. Shadow, Glow & Outline: *Strength* is *Spread*, the last controls are in
  *Placement and scenes*, and every control has a tooltip. Auto Frame hides the frame's width, *Fit*
  and alignment with *Just the image*, shows *Saved aspect ratios* only for the automatic trim, and
  every control has a tooltip.
- **Show in Scenes and Program Trigger grouped.** Show in Scenes: *Sources*, *Each press*, the
  transition, then *Apply the switch now*. Program Trigger: a second group, *2. Show and switch on*;
  its transition hides on a source, where it does nothing, and the title box offers the open
  windows' titles.
- **Morph's window has headings.** *Sources that match*, *Sources with no partner*, *Links* and the
  rules. Controls that do nothing for the current choice hide, and as a source's own transition the
  scene-change settings hide. In a filter's *Show/Hide transition* the Morph links sit inside the
  group, and an *Unpaired transition* line heads the copied settings.
- **Ambient Background in smaller groups.** *Background*, *Live capture*, *Capture change*, *Blur
  and mosaic*, *Light and colour* and *Darkest level*. *Glow* is *Midtone boost*; source lists start
  with *(none)* and leave out the background itself; every control has a tooltip.
- **The dock.** *Move in every scene* (Place) and *Repeat in every scene* (Arrange) have their own
  names; the preview menu shows the Arrange one. The Copy tab starts with *Chosen scenes* and
  *Chosen sources*, then *Copy transform*. Each number of *On drop* sits under its tick box.
  The Standby & Ambient tab shows the standby image, with the background's own image in a part
  of its own, and *Background settings…* opens the background of the scene being edited.

## Good to know

- No setting, id or saved choice changed: every filter you have reads as before. Only the order,
  grouping and visibility of controls changed.
- Not yet seen in OBS on Windows; the windows were opened and checked headless.
