# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Scene Transitions can now have the last word on how every source in its scene shows and hides.

## New

- **Override other filters** in Scene Transitions (off as it comes). Tick it and every source the
  filter covers shows and hides with the scene's transition (or the source's own, under *Settings
  for*), even when a Link Visibility, Show in Scenes, Scene Modes, Program Trigger or window
  switcher is set to a transition of its own. A source a Standby Image stands in for still uses the
  Standby's transition.

## Changed

- **The list under *Sources covered* now says what it is for.** With *All sources* it is called
  *Sources with own settings*: add a source there to give it a transition of its own, and choose it
  under *Settings for*. With *Chosen sources* it is called *Chosen sources*: only those sources get
  the scene's transition. The heading and its tooltip change as soon as you switch.

## Good to know

- **Why the scene's transitions seemed to do nothing:** a filter set to a transition of its own
  (a Link Visibility or Show in Scenes set to Morph, say) uses it for every change it makes, and
  the scene's transition only comes back afterwards. Set those filters' *Show transition* to *As
  the scene sets*, or tick *Override other filters*.
- **Morph's choices in Scene Transitions:** choose Morph as the show or hide transition and its
  options appear right under it: *Unpaired sources* (how a source with nothing to change into
  comes and goes), *Morph the main image*, *Use visible pixels*, *Ignore shadows & outlines* and
  the easing, with *Linked sources* below to say which sources change into each other.
- Not yet tried on Windows.
