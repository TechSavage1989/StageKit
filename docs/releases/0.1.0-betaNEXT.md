# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Outlines get corner shapes and match between the source and scene filters, stay solid through a Morph, and glows fall off smoothly with a new Intensity.

## New

- **Outline corners.** Shadow, Glow & Outline and Scene Shadow have *Outline corners*: *Follow
  the source* (as before), or *Rounded*, *Bevel*, *Notch* or *Scoop* with a *Corner size*, which
  draw the line as a shape round the source's box. The drop shadow takes the same shape, and Morph
  carries it.
- **Glow intensity.** *Intensity* makes a glow or shadow denser and brighter without a hard edge
  (100 % is as before).

## Changed

- **Smoother glows and shadows.** Soft shadows and glows are blurred in more precision and fall
  off smoothly, with no visible steps at the end of their soft tail. Their look is very close to
  before; the very end of the tail is a little softer.

## Fixed

- **Scene Shadow's outline and shadow match the source filter's.** Scene Shadow's drop shadow is
  now thrown by the sources with their outline, as Shadow, Glow & Outline's always was: the same
  width looks the same in both, and the shadow behind a rounded outline is rounded too, not
  square.
- **Outlines stay solid through a Morph.** Two different sources changing into each other (one
  title into another, say) keep a solid outline and shadow all the way, instead of going
  see-through where one of them has nothing.

## Good to know

- **Only run on OpenGL.** The shader changes have only been run on OpenGL here, not on Direct3D,
  and have not been seen on Windows.
- **Which scenes change.** A Scene Shadow with both a drop shadow and an outline now shows a
  little more shadow beyond the outline, as the per-source filter does.
