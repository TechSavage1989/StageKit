# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Six new animated patterns built on hexagons and dots, for Ambient Background and, five of them, for outlines.

## New

- **Hex columns.** Six-sided columns seen from above and in front, with lit tops, darker sides and
  dark edges, rising and falling in slow waves. Ambient Background only.
- **Mesh.** Perforated metal: a tight comb of round discs with dark rims, under a wide soft light
  that sweeps across.
- **Halftone hexagons.** Fine dots in two tones whose size draws large hexagons with rings in
  them; a wave passes from hexagon to hexagon while the whole turns very slowly.
- **Dot grid.** Rows of small glowing dots under diagonal beams of light. A dot under a beam is
  larger and brighter, and where two beams cross it flares in a four-point star.
- **Hex wire glow.** A dark honeycomb with thin lines that light up where a soft light passes,
  one colour at one side of the screen and another at the other.
- **Hex mosaic.** Flat hexagon tiles in close shades that shimmer slowly. Calm and low in
  contrast.
- **Five of them as outline fills.** *Outline design* in Shadow, Glow & Outline and Scene Shadow
  gains *Pattern: Mesh*, *Halftone*, *Dot grid*, *Hex wire glow* and *Hex mosaic*.

## Good to know

- **Colour 1 is the ground.** In all six, Colour 1 is the ground, dark or light, and Colours 2
  and 3 are what is lit or inked. They are made for a light ground as well as a dark one.
- **Turn Pattern size down in an outline.** At 30 to 50 % the discs, dots and cells fit in a
  line's width.
- **Everything else works on them.** Speed, Size, Detail, Position X, Position Y and Rotation,
  the blur, the mosaic and bloom act on the new patterns as on the others, and each comes back to
  where it began every four minutes with no jump.
- **A file of their own.** The new patterns are in a shader file of their own. If a graphics
  driver refuses it, the older patterns still work and OBS's log says so once.
