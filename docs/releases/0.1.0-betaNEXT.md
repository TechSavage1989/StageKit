# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Outlines can be filled with a moving pattern, and there are seven new patterns, a circuit board among them.

## New

- **Pattern outlines.** *Outline design*, in Shadow, Glow & Outline and in Scene Shadow, has
  fifteen new designs that fill the line with a moving pattern: Aurora, Plasma, Lava lamp, Waves,
  Nebula, Gradient flow, Starfield, Bokeh, Rays, Circuit board, Hex grid, Matrix rain, Tunnel,
  Ripples and Embers.
- **Circuit board.** A new pattern for Ambient Background and for outlines: a board with traces,
  pads and chips, and lights running along the traces. Colour 1 is the board, Colour 2 the
  traces, Colour 3 the lights; *Detail* sets how much is on the board.
- **Six more patterns.** Hex grid (a honeycomb whose cells light up), Matrix rain, Tunnel,
  Ripples, Embers and Sun grid (a sun over a grid running to the horizon), all in Ambient
  Background's *Pattern* list with the same colours, Speed, Size and Detail.
- **The line keeps its shape.** Width, corners and *Follow the source* work as before; the line is
  a window onto the pattern, and the drop shadow is still thrown by the whole line.
- **Your colours, size and speed.** The outline colour, the *Second colour* and a new *Third
  colour* are the pattern's palette. *Pattern size* makes it smaller or larger, and *Speed* moves
  it (0 holds it still, a negative number runs it backwards).
- **Lines that flow together.** In Scene Shadow the pattern lies over the whole scene, so the
  outlines of sources side by side are parts of one moving pattern.

## Good to know

- In Shadow, Glow & Outline the pattern lies over the source itself and travels with it; only
  Scene Shadow knows where a source is on screen.
- Plasma, Nebula and Gradient flow colour a thin line all the way round. Aurora, Starfield, Rays,
  Ripples and Embers are mostly the outline colour with light passing through; they look best on
  a wide line.
- For Circuit board, Hex grid and Matrix rain in an outline, turn *Pattern size* down (to about
  30 %) so that traces, cells and streaks fit in the line's width.
- Grid pulse and Sun grid are not offered as outlines: in a thin line the first is only a tick
  now and then, and the second is a view with a horizon rather than a texture.
- Solid and the other designs are drawn exactly as before, and saved filters and backgrounds
  keep what they had.
- The patterns' shaders have only run on OpenGL here and have not been seen on Windows. If they
  fail to load there, a pattern outline is drawn in its outline colour, solid (OBS's log has the
  line *Outline patterns are not available*), and a pattern background shows the standby image.
  The seven new patterns are in a shader file of their own, so the first ten do not depend on
  them.
