# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

An outline no longer shows a thin line along the screen's edge when its source fills the screen.

## Fixed

- **No outline sliver at the screen's edge.** A source with a Shadow, Glow & Outline line, drawn
  larger than its own size (a 1920x1080 capture filling a 2560x1440 screen, say) and fitted to the
  whole screen, showed a pixel of its line along one or more edges of the screen, though the whole
  line lay off it. The source's own pixels now show nothing of the line beside them.

## Good to know

- **A cropped source is not covered yet.** OBS draws a cropped item, or one with a scale filter
  or a blending mode, in a way of its own; such an item may still show the sliver.
