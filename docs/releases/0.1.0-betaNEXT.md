# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

A source that fades in or out under Scene Shadow now fades with its outline and shadow as one image.

## Fixed

- **Fading sources no longer look doubled or see-through against their outline.** In Scene Shadow,
  a source that fades in or out (Morph's *Other items* and *Unpaired sources*, Make room with
  *Fade while moving*, or a source's own show or hide transition) had its outline and shadow laid
  under it as well, so the image was darkened and the line seemed to show through it. The source,
  its outline and its shadow now fade together as one flat image; a source that moves without
  fading keeps a solid line, and sources that morph into each other are unchanged.

## Good to know

- Tested on OpenGL only. The change adds shader code that has not yet run on Windows (Direct3D);
  should Scene Shadow or outlines look wrong after updating, please send the OBS log.
