# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

A Show in Scenes switch is now a control that turns its sources on or off when you press it, and
nothing more.

## Changed

- **A Show in Scenes switch no longer stops other filters from showing its source.** Before, a
  switch that was off kept its source hidden: Link Visibility (*Follow sources*) and Scene Modes
  would not show it. Now a switch only acts when it is pressed. Afterwards, any
  other filter may show or hide the source again.
- **A source turned off with its switch can be brought back by those filters.** For example, a
  camera switched off comes back when a Link Visibility that follows Intermission Text shows it, or
  when a Scene Modes mode that includes it comes on. Press the switch off again to hide it, or take
  the source out of that link or mode if it should stay off there.

## Unchanged

- Pressing a switch still shows or hides its sources in the scenes it covers.
- *Follow what is shown* still only turns the switch on or off to match what is shown; it never
  shows or hides anything.
- A switch still does not show a source in a mode that is not showing: Scene Modes and Link
  Visibility's *Follow sources* keep the sources of other modes hidden, as before.

## Good to know

- In your holding scene, the DSLR now shows with Intermission Text even while its capture switch
  reads off.
- Not yet tried on Windows.
