# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Scene Shadow no longer flickers when you show or hide sources, and text keeps its shadow right
against its edges.

## Fixed

- **Scene Shadow no longer flickers when sources are shown or hidden with Morph.** When one source
  changed into another with Morph as its show or hide transition, the shadow of the two vanished
  for up to a quarter of a second as the change began, then came back. The shadow now stays on
  through the whole change. A source added to a scene on screen also has its shadow from the
  first frame.
- **No bright line between a source and its scene shadow.** Along smoothed edges, text above all,
  a thin line of the background showed between each letter and its shadow. The shadow now reaches
  right up to the edge, with *Shadow from* set to *The scene as drawn* or *Each source by itself*.
- **Removing a Scene Shadow could crash OBS.** Taking the filter off a scene at the moment the
  scene was being drawn could close OBS. It no longer does.

## Unchanged

- Fade, Slide and other show and hide transitions, changes of scene with Morph, and Fit to Space
  moves looked right before and still do.
- Your Scene Shadow settings are kept as they are.
