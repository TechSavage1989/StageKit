# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

StageKit Views: image-only buttons show their name like Stream Deck keys, and the panel settings now sit behind a ⚙ button at the bottom.

## New

- **Titles on image-only buttons.** Image-only tiles show their name as a Stream Deck-style title, in the dark band at the bottom by default. Choose **Top**, **Middle**, **Bottom** or **Hidden** and **Small**, **Medium** or **Large** in the panel settings.
- **A title position per button.** In **Edit panel...**, a button's **Title** can differ from the panel's choice.

## Changed

- **Panel settings behind ⚙, at the bottom.** StageKit Views shows its controls with one bar at the bottom: the **Scene** selector and a ⚙ button. Click ⚙ to show **Edit panel...**, **Manage controllers...**, **View**, **Button size**, **Image only** and the title choices above it.

## Good to know

- Buttons smaller than 32 pixels show no title; hover for the name. Existing panels keep their layout, size and image-only choice.
