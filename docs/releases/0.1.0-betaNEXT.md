# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

Make Room: sources slide off the screen to make room for what morphs in.

## New

- **Make Room (StageKit)**, a transition. It is Morph, with one difference: whatever has no partner
  in the other scene slides right off the screen instead of fading. Each source goes by the screen
  edge nearest to it among the edges that lie away from what is morphing in: a panel left of a game
  that grows into the middle leaves by the left edge; a badge above and to the right of it leaves by
  the top or the right edge, whichever is nearer, and never the bottom. Sources that are only in the
  new scene come in the same way, from the edge away from where the morph comes from. With nothing
  morphing, a source simply takes its nearest edge. Backgrounds still cross-fade.
- **Make room in Morph.** The Morph transition's *Other items* (for a change of scene) and
  *Unpaired sources* (when Morph is a source's own show or hide transition) both have *Make room*.
  With Morph between items, a source left over without a partner now slides off the screen, away
  from the pair changing over beside it, instead of fading in place.
- **Make Room for a single source.** Chosen as a source's show or hide transition (in Show in
  Scenes, Link Visibility, Scene Transitions, or the source's own *Show Transition* / *Hide
  Transition*), the source slides off the screen when hidden and in from it when shown: away from
  whatever else is shown or hidden in that scene at the same moment, or by its nearest edge when
  nothing else changes. It travels across the whole screen, not only inside its own frame.
- Two settings: **Make room by** (*Nearest edge away from the morph*, as it comes, or *Nearest
  edge only*) and **Fade while moving** (off as it comes).

## Good to know

- As a single source's transition, Make Room starts about a tenth of a second after the source is
  shown or hidden, so it can see what else changes with it. In a scene that is not on screen, or
  for a source inside a group, the source slides inside its own frame instead.
- OBS's log has one line per source that makes room, naming the edge and why.
