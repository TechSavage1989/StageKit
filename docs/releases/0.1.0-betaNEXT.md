# StageKit 0.1.0-betaNEXT

The dock can fit an item to the free space around it in one press, and open OBS's Edit Transform window.

## New

- **Fit to space in one press.** A new button on the dock's Place tab makes the selected item as
  large as the free space around it allows, keeping its shape, the edge distance short of the
  screen's edges and of everything else, measured by visible pixels and without shadows when those
  are ticked. It glides there, is one step for Undo, and follows *In every scene*. Also in the
  preview's right-click menu, as a hotkey and on the Stream Deck *Arrange tool* key.
- **Fit to space never pushes fitted sources around.** A source a Fit to Space or Scene Fit looks
  after, shown or hidden, keeps its place and size: the tool stays out of the whole space that
  filter holds, and a source laid out by hand that its filter keeps where it was put stays there.
  If that filter would still have to move or resize its source, the item is kept
  smaller, or left as it is, and the status line says why. An item whose own fit filter resizes it
  is left to that filter. With *In every scene*, a scene where the copy would push a fitted source
  is left out and named, and so is a scene not shown since OBS started.
- **Edit transform button.** Opens OBS's own Edit Transform window for the item chosen in the dock,
  selecting it in OBS's preview first, from the Place tab.

## Changed

- **The edge distance is remembered.** The Place tab's *Edge distance* is now kept with the scene
  collection, like the other dock options.

## Good to know

- Something the item lies wholly on, such as a background, is not in the way; anything it only
  overlaps is, and it is fitted beside it.
- An item in a group that a Fit to Space fits is left as it is: changing it would change the group.
- Stream Deck plugin 0.33.0.0: the Arrange tool key has *Fit to space*.
- Not yet tried on Windows.
