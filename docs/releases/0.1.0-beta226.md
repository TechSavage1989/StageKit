# StageKit 0.1.0-beta226

Outlines can show an Ambient Background: its pattern, image or live capture, blurred and mosaicked as the background is.

## New

- **Ambient Background as an outline.** *Outline design* in Shadow, Glow & Outline and Scene
  Shadow gains *Ambient Background*: the line shows what an Ambient Background shows, with its
  blur, mosaic, bloom and transitions, and moves as it moves. Borders match the background
  behind them, and a live capture blended into the background shows in the borders too.
- **Choose the background.** *Background* is *Automatic* as it comes (the one in the scene, or
  any there is), or any Ambient Background by name, even one in no scene. A renamed background
  is followed.
- **Fill brightness and Fill saturation.** The line is lifted (130 % and 120 % as they come) so
  it can be told from the background where the two meet; at 100 % it is exactly the background.

## Changed

- **Laid out like a pattern.** *Pattern size*, *Position X*, *Position Y* and *Rotation* move,
  size and turn the background inside the line. In Scene Shadow the line shows the background
  where it lies on the screen; in Shadow, Glow & Outline the background's middle sits on the
  source's middle and travels with it.
- **The shadow and glow keep their own colours.** Only the outline takes the background.
- **Nothing changes by itself.** Filters saved before keep their outline design; the new
  settings only act when *Ambient Background* is chosen.
- **The background is not drawn twice.** The line reuses the picture the background has just
  drawn; a background in no scene on show is drawn once a frame for all the lines that use it.

## Fixed

- **Scene Shadow outlines on OpenGL.** Neon and patterned outlines now share a texture binding, keeping the canvas-edge correction and restoring the glow on OpenGL systems affected by beta225.
