# StageKit 0.1.0-beta162

One hundred and sixty-second pre-release: **All sources** in the scene filters, **Space priority**
for fitted sources, more show and hide transitions for **Link Visibility**, and a crash fix. Tested on
Linux only. The Stream Deck plugin is unchanged (0.21.0.0).

## Added

- **All sources in the scene filters.** Each scene filter that works on chosen sources now starts
  its *Sources covered* list with **All sources**: every source in the scene, a group counting as
  one, and sources you add later too. Sources you never want touched go under **Ignored sources**.
  - **Scene Transitions**: *All sources* or *Chosen sources*. (*Leave these alone* is now called
    *Ignored sources*.)
  - **Scene Fit**: *All sources* or *Chosen sources*, and a new **Ignored sources** list. With All
    sources, every source is fitted, each with its own settings under *Settings for*. The background
    (a source that fills nearly the whole screen) is left alone, and so is a source that already has
    its own Fit to Space. A source you put down on top of a fitted one waits until that one has made
    room, then is fitted into the room.
  - **Scene Standby**: *All sources*, *Every capture* or *Chosen sources*.
  - **Scene Corners**: *All sources*, *All but the background* or *Chosen sources*.
  - **Scene Shadow**: *All sources* or *Chosen sources*. (*No shadow for* is now called *Ignored
    sources*.)
- **Space priority**, on every Fit to Space and on each source of a Scene Fit: which source gets the
  room when two want the same space.
  - **Takes space first**: this source takes the room it would have if the others were not there,
    and never moves or shrinks for them. The others fit around it. Use it for your game or main
    capture.
  - **Shares space**: as it always was. The sources make room for each other.
  - **Makes room**: this source always fits around the others.
  - **Priority by list order** (a tick box): ticked, the selector is greyed out and the order of the
    filters decides instead: a filter higher in the scene's filter list takes its space first. Move
    a filter up or down and the sources glide to their new places. On a Scene Fit, the order of its
    *Fitted sources* list decides, and with *All sources* the sources not in that list come after
    it, from the top of the scene down.
  - It works with *All sources*: each source a Scene Fit finds by itself has its own *Space priority*
    too.
- **Link Visibility's show and hide transitions.** Link Visibility now has the same fuller
  transition controls as Show in Scenes: a different transition for showing and for hiding, each
  transition's own settings (the direction of a slide, for example), and *Settings for*, where one
  source can be given a transition of its own.
- **Morph: Unpaired sources.** When Morph is a source's show or hide transition and that source has
  nothing to change into, you now choose how it comes and goes: *Fade*, *Fade and zoom*, *Slide off
  the nearest edge*, a *Cut*, or any other transition you have, with that transition's own settings.
  You find it in Morph's own settings and, under Morph, in Show in Scenes and Link Visibility. Only a
  source that really has no partner uses it: sources that pair up, are linked, or join another
  change still morph as before.

## Fixed

- **A crash in a filter's settings window.** A settings window with the fuller transition controls
  could crash OBS while it was being opened, when one list rebuilt the controls under it. It was found
  with the new *Unpaired sources* list, and the transition lists had the same weakness. The controls
  are now only rebuilt when what they show has actually changed.

## Your existing scenes stay as they were

- Filters you already have keep covering what they covered: a Scene Fit stays on its chosen sources,
  Scene Standby on every capture, Scene Corners on all but the background, Scene Shadow on all
  sources, and Scene Transitions on whichever of the two it was set to. Nothing switches to *All
  sources* by itself, and new filters start where they always did.
- Every Fit to Space and Scene Fit you already have is on **Shares space** with *Priority by list
  order* unticked, so your layouts fit exactly as before. New filters start the same way; you choose
  where one source should win.
- Link Visibility filters keep the transition and duration they had; hiding uses the same transition
  as showing until you change it.
- Morph's unpaired sources fade, as before. A Morph you had set to *Fade and zoom* for other items
  still fades and zooms.

## Not yet tried on Windows

- None of this release has run on Windows yet. In particular:
  - how *All sources* settles in your real layout scenes, and Scene Fit's settings window with many
    sources, each with its own *Space priority*;
  - *Space priority* with your Live and Chat Live scenes;
  - another plugin's transition chosen under *Unpaired sources* (only OBS's Slide and StageKit's Fade
    and Zoom have been tried).
