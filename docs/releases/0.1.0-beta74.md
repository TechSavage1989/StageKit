# StageKit 0.1.0-beta74

Seventy-fourth pre-release: snap and the resize limit glide, and a fix aimed at choppy moves. It
passes the automated tests on Linux; **it has not been run in OBS on Windows yet.**

## Changed

- **Snap on release and "Stop resizing at edges" glide into place** instead of jumping. They use the
  dock's own glide time and curve (the ones under *Send to*); a glide time of 0 jumps as before.
  Taking hold of the item again stops its glide. Undo is unchanged.

## Fixed

- **Choppy moves, most likely.** Fit Item's *The free space around it* looked at the other items'
  pictures four times a second, and at all of them again after anything moved, and each look holds
  the picture up for a moment. It now does that once per item, follows an item that moves or changes
  size by its box at no cost, and looks again only one item a second while nothing is moving.
  - The fitted item now follows a moving camera as it moves, instead of waiting for it to stop.
  - A widget that starts showing something after showing nothing is noticed within a few seconds
    rather than within one.
- *Keep it on screen* (Shadow, Glow & Outline) looks at its items a few times a second instead of
  on every frame.

I could not measure the choppiness here, so this is the likeliest cause, not a proven one.

## Please check

1. Move the camera with its buttons: is the move smooth again?
2. If not: set Fit Item back to *The whole screen* for a moment and say whether that makes it smooth.
3. Drag an item near an edge with *Snap on release* on: it should glide the last bit.
