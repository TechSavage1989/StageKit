# StageKit 0.1.0-beta161

One hundred and sixty-first pre-release: a seventh scene filter, **Scene Blur**, and a **Scene Blur**
key for the Stream Deck. Tested on Linux only.

## Added

- **Scene Blur.** A blur for a scene's sources, in that scene only. Switch it on for a "be right
  back" over the game, say, and the same game in your other scenes stays sharp. Add it to the scene
  (right-click the scene, *Filters*).
  - **Sources to blur**: *All sources*, *Live and capture sources* (game, window, display and camera
    captures, capture cards and videos), or *Chosen sources*, which you list under *Blur these*.
  - **Never blur these**: sources that always stay sharp, whatever is chosen above. Your camera, for
    example.
  - A sharp source sitting on top of a blurred one stays sharp, and its colours do not smear into the
    blur around it.
  - The same blur settings as the Blur filter: Gaussian, Dual Kawase, Box, the mosaics with their
    seven cell shapes and glass, Zoom, Motion and Trail, strength up to 1000, and how long it takes
    to blur in and out, with easing.
  - Switch it with the eye, a Stream Deck key, or three hotkeys under the scene in *Settings,
    Hotkeys*: *StageKit: Scene Blur Toggle*, *On* and *Off*. A blur that was on when OBS closed is
    on again when OBS starts, without blurring in.
  - It moves nothing and adds nothing to your sources. Switched off, it costs nothing.
  - It works with Scene Shadow in either order: it puts itself after the shadow by itself, and the
    shadows are blurred with what they fall on.
- **Stream Deck plugin 0.21.0.0: a Scene Blur key.** Choose the *Blur filter* by name, then the
  *Scene*: one scene, or *All scenes with this filter* to switch every scene that has a Scene Blur of
  that name at once. *On press*: *Toggle*, *Switch on* or *Switch off*. The key is lit while the blur
  is on (with all scenes, while any of them is), however it was switched. Its image follows its
  *Blur style* list, the same fourteen styles as the Blur key, or the blur the filter is set to.

## Good to know

- **Morph cross-fades while the blur is on.** A change of scene to or from a scene whose Scene Blur
  is blurring is a plain cross-fade, so the blur stays on screen the whole way. With the blur off,
  the scene morphs as it always has.
- **The blur stays inside each source's outline.** A Blur filter on the source itself spreads a
  little past the source's edge; Scene Blur keeps the blur within the shape of what shows. A group
  or nested scene is blurred, or kept sharp, as one source.
