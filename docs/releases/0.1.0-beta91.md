# StageKit 0.1.0-beta91

Ninety-first pre-release: a tidier dock and one new setting, all asked for by the owner. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## New

- **Fit to Space, *The free space around it*: Tolerance.** When something sticks a little way into
  the space, the picture can either be made that much smaller and keep the whole space, or keep its
  size and lose the part of the space beside that thing. *Tolerance* says how many pixels smaller is
  still fine: up to that, it keeps the whole space. It comes set to 8 px, which is what beta88 did.
  At 0 the biggest picture always wins, even by one pixel, as before beta88. The gap is kept either
  way.

## Changed in the dock

- **Arrange tab:** the line-up, spacing, snap and size tools are drawings alone, six to a row. Point
  at one for its name and what it does. The four order tools are one row: to the front, forward
  one, back one, to the back.
- **Place tab:** the nine arrows are a block of squares. Beside them, in the room that leaves:
  *Slide to the next edge*, *In every scene*, *Use visible pixels* and *Ignore shadows & outlines*.
- The update menu at the bottom is the word **Updates**, not three dots.
- *Fit to Space* works its space out again as soon as its gap is changed.
