# StageKit 0.1.0-beta152

One hundred and fifty-second pre-release: the StageKit Background dock is now a tab of the StageKit
dock. Tested on Linux only.

## Changed

- **Background tab.** The standby image, its thumbnail, *Choose the standby image…*, *Background
  image…* and *Background settings…* are on a fifth tab, *Background*, of the StageKit dock. The
  separate StageKit Background dock is gone; if you had it open somewhere, that space closes up.
