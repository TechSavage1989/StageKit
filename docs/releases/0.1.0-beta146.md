# StageKit 0.1.0-beta146

One hundred and forty-sixth pre-release: Blur, Zoom and Auto Frame may come after Standby Image.
Tested on Linux only.

## Changed

- **Any StageKit filter can now sit after Standby Image** on a source that uses *Show when source
  is hidden*. Blur, Zoom and Auto Frame draw their input afresh each time, as Shadow, Glow &
  Outline and Corners & Edges have since beta142, so the source and its standby image stay apart
  through the transition whichever of them follows.
  - What still has to come before Standby Image: OBS's own filters (colour correction, crop and
    the like) and other plugins' filters. OBS keeps one image per filter per frame and StageKit
    cannot change that for filters that are not its own.
  - Nothing changes in how Blur, Zoom and Auto Frame look.
