# StageKit 0.1.0-beta157

One hundred and fifty-seventh pre-release: switching from the full-screen camera to a capture
morphs, as switching between two captures does. Tested on Linux only.

## Fixed

- **A capture morphs out of the full-screen camera.** In a scene where showing a capture hides a
  full-screen camera and brings back the camera's corner view (Live: DSLR, Window or Game Capture,
  Square Camera), the two cameras, linked in Morph's settings, changed into each other and the
  capture had nothing left to change from: up to beta155 it popped in at the end, and in beta156 it
  faded in where it is. Now a source left over still changes into (or out of) one that is already
  changing, by the same rules (linked sources, then the main image). The camera goes into its
  corner view and the capture comes out of the camera; switching back, both go into the camera. It
  works whichever order the changes arrive in, and a small source with no link still fades by
  itself.

## Not yet tried on Windows

- Tested headless with OBS 30; not yet run in OBS 32 on Windows.
