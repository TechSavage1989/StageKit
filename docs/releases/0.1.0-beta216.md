# StageKit 0.1.0-beta216

Circuit board is redrawn with ribbons of traces and lights that run along them, and two more circuit patterns join it: Circuit traces and Data lines.

## New

- **Circuit traces, a new pattern.** Bundles of thin traces come down from the top, step sideways
  together and each end in a round pad at its own height. Lights run down them and the pad lights
  as one arrives; small lights drift up. In Ambient Background's *Pattern* list and as an
  *Outline design*.
- **Data lines, a new pattern.** A few very thin lines run across with gentle steps, most of them
  faint and a few bright, with dashes of light sliding along them and small drifting lights in
  the third colour. Calm and sparse. In the *Pattern* list and as an *Outline design*.

## Changed

- **Circuit board looks and moves like a board now.** Ribbons of parallel traces run a long way
  and bend together at 45°, each trace ends in a ring pad, a few thick traces cross, and a dimmer
  board lies under it. Lights run along the ribbons, several at once and now and then a whole
  ribbon together, and the pads light as they arrive; a slow sheen passes over. Turn *Detail* up
  for wider ribbons, chips, soldered pads and pours; down for a clean, sparse board. A background
  or outline already set to Circuit board gets the new look.
- **The three circuit patterns work on light boards too.** Colour 1 is the board, Colour 2 the
  lines, Colour 3 what lights up. With a light Colour 1 (cream, pale grey, pink) the lights turn
  deeper instead of whiter.

## Good to know

- The three circuit patterns have only been run on OpenGL here. If one shows the standby image
  instead on Windows, OBS's log has a line naming `stagekit-patterns-circuit.effect`: please send
  it. The other patterns are in other files and are not affected.
- In an outline, turn *Pattern size* down to 30 % or so for Circuit board and Circuit traces.
