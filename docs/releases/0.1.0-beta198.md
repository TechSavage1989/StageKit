# StageKit 0.1.0-beta198

Fit to space follows each source's alignment and gap, and handheld captures keep their image size when their resolution changes.

## New

## Changed

- **Fit entry settings in the dock.** Fit to space uses the source's alignment, gap and measuring options even while its fit filter is off.
- **Measurement status.** The dock tells you when OBS was busy and the fit needs another press.

## Fixed

- **Free space beside a column.** Scene Fit and the dock use visible images to find room. Hidden layouts and standby twins leave their space available.
- **Resize to fit.** An undersized image grows into the available space. An image already laid out to fit keeps its position within the configured tolerance.
- **Kept image size.** A capture keeps its on-screen image dimensions across resolution changes. A different shape fits within those dimensions.

## Good to know

- **Older collections.** Kept sizes are converted when the capture supplies a usable image.
