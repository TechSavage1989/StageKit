# StageKit 0.1.0-beta155

One hundred and fifty-fifth pre-release: switching from the full-screen camera to a capture morphs
again. Tested on Linux only.

## Fixed

- **A capture morphs out of the full-screen camera again.** In a scene where showing a capture
  hides a full-screen camera and brings back the camera's corner view (Live: DSLR, Window or Game
  Capture, Square Camera), the two cameras, linked in Morph's settings, changed into each other and
  the capture only faded in by itself. Now an item left over still changes into (or out of) one
  that is already changing, by the same rules (linked sources, then the main image): the camera
  goes into its corner view and the capture comes out of the camera, and switching back, both go
  into the camera. It works whichever order the changes arrive in, and a small item with no link
  still fades by itself.
