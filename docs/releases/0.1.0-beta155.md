# StageKit 0.1.0-beta155

One hundred and fifty-fifth pre-release: six **scene filters**, each one filter on a scene that looks
after all of that scene's sources, and a **Scene Mode** key for the Stream Deck. Tested on Linux only.

The work to bring the frame time down, and a new scene collection built with these filters, come
in the next beta (beta156). Nothing in your current collection changes until you add a scene filter
to a scene.

## Added

A scene filter goes on a scene, not on a source: right-click the scene, *Filters*, and add it there.
One filter takes the place of one per source.

- **Scene Modes.** Give a scene modes, such as *Game*, *Window* and *Display* in a live scene, or
  *Starting*, *Be right back*, *Intermission* and *Ending* in a standby scene, and choose the sources
  of each. One mode is on at a time: turning a mode on shows its sources and hides the other modes'.
  A source in both modes stays as it is, and a source in no mode is never touched. Make modes by hand
  or from a template; no names are built in, and renaming a mode breaks no key. Each mode can
  *Switch to scene*, other scenes can follow the same modes (*Same modes in scenes*), and a camera
  switched off stays off. Every mode has a hotkey, and there is one for the next mode.
- **Scene Transitions.** One place to set how every source in a scene shows and hides, instead of a
  transition setting inside each filter. It sets OBS's own show and hide transitions, so they also
  hold for the eye, hotkeys and Stream Deck *Source Visibility* keys. A source can have its own
  settings, and you can list sources to leave alone.
- **Scene Fit.** Fit to Space for several sources of a scene at once. They are worked out together
  and all move at the same moment, so a camera being hidden or a game changing shape no longer makes
  the others shift a few times before they settle. *Take over Fit to Space* turns the scene's Fit to
  Space filters into entries here, with all their settings, and switches them off (not deleted).
- **Scene Standby.** The standby image set once for a scene: every empty capture shows it, and any
  source you list shows it in its place while hidden (in that scene only). It adds a Standby Image
  filter to each capture, marked *Set by Scene Standby*; a Standby Image you made yourself is left
  alone.
- **Scene Corners.** The same corners and edges on every source of a scene, set once, leaving out the
  full-screen background. It adds a Corners & Edges filter to each source, marked *Set by Scene
  Corners*. Nothing changes size, so nothing moves.
- **Scene Shadow.** One shadow, glow or outline for a whole scene. It falls on the background and
  never on a source, and no source is resized or moved, so Fit to Space, the arrange tools and Morph
  work as they do without it. **Morph still morphs** a scene with a Scene Shadow, and the shadow goes
  with the change. Sources that overlap make one shape; where a shadow must fall on another source (a
  camera over a game), keep a Shadow, Glow & Outline on that source and Scene Shadow leaves it alone.
- **Stream Deck plugin 0.18.0.0:**
  - A **Source type** list on every key that acts on a source and on the Scene Mode key: *Automatic*,
    then *Game*, *Window*, *Art app*, *Code editor*, *Browser*, *Camera*, *Capture card*, *Display*,
    *Phone*, *Tablet*, *Chat*, *Music*, *Alerts*, *Title / text*, *Image*, *Video*, *Starting*,
    *Be right back*, *Intermission* and *Ending*. Choose what your source is, whatever it is called,
    and the key shows that image. An image you choose yourself still comes first.
  - The new **Scene Mode** key turns on one mode of a scene's Scene Modes (*On press*: *Switch on* or
    *Toggle*). It is lit while its mode is on, however it was turned on, and takes the mode's name as
    its title.
  - On and off keys for **Scene Fit**, **Scene Shadow**, **Scene Corners** and **Scene Standby**, and
    the *Fit to Space Overlap* key works on a Scene Fit too.

## Changed

- **As the scene sets** is a new transition choice, and now the one every filter that shows and hides
  sources starts with: Show in Scenes, Link Visibility, Program Trigger, the two switchers and
  Standby Image. In a scene with a Scene Transitions, the change is the one set
  there. In a scene without one, it is a fade of the filter's own length, which is what these filters
  did before, so nothing looks different until you add a Scene Transitions.
- A Standby Image filter no longer makes its image again when only its timing changes.
- Fit to Space does less work in scenes that are not on screen (it looks at its source four times a
  second there, and puts everything right the moment the scene comes on).

## Not yet tried on Windows

- Scene Shadow's shader has only run on OpenGL. Windows compiles it when OBS starts. If OBS's log has
  an error about `stagekit-scene-shadow.effect`, the scene is drawn without its shadow: please send the
  log.
- The scene filters' settings windows in OBS on Windows, Morph between two captures as a Scene Modes
  change, and the Stream Deck settings pages and Scene Mode key on a real deck.
