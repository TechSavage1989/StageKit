# StageKit 0.1.0-beta117

One hundred and seventeenth pre-release: Capture App in Use switches at once, has Morph, apps you
switch to by hand, and can hide the capture. It passes the automated tests on Linux; the Windows
side has not run in a real OBS yet.

## New in Capture App in Use

- **Much faster switching.** *Keep the others ready* (ticked as it comes): while the capture is
  showing, the other open apps on its lists are captured in the background, so the app you switch
  to is there at once instead of after a pause. It costs what a capture per app costs, as in your
  present scene; untick it to go back to one capture that takes a moment to find the window.
- **Morph** under *Change with*: the frame changes shape and size as the one app's picture turns
  into the other's.
- **Apps you switch to**: a second list, for a browser and the like. *Switch to the next of these*
  holds the next one that is open, whatever app you click into; after the last, or with *Back to
  following the apps*, your apps are followed again.
- **Hiding and showing it**: hides the capture in every scene, or shows it again, with any
  transition (Morph and Fade and Zoom among them).
- **Hotkeys** for all three under the capture in *Settings, Hotkeys*, and buttons in the filter.
- **Stream Deck plugin 0.8.0.0**: the *Next Manual Capture* key now also takes a Capture App in
  Use (the list is called *Scene or capture*), and *On press* has *Next capture or app*, *Back to
  automatic* and *Hide or show the capture*.

## Fixed

- **The flash between two apps.** The one capture let go of the first window before it had the
  second, and that moment showed. With *Keep the others ready* the new app's picture comes from a
  capture that is already running, and the filter does not go back to the capture's own picture
  until that has settled on the new window.
- The filter did not stay first in the capture's filter list: it now keeps itself before a Standby
  Picture and an Auto Frame.
- The status line said nothing was being captured while one of the apps was. It now says what the
  capture is aimed at.
- It notices a change of app sooner (it looked five times a second; now twenty).

## Please check

1. Click from one of your apps to another. Is the switch as quick as with Show Source in Use, and
   has OBS's frame time stayed where it was?
2. Choose *Morph* under *Change with*. Does the picture change shape smoothly?
3. Add your browser under *Apps you switch to* and press *Switch to the next of these*. Does it
   hold the browser while you click into your apps, and go back when pressed again?
4. Press *Hide or show it now*. Does the capture hide and come back?
