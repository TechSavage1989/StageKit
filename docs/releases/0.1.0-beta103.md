# StageKit 0.1.0-beta103

One hundred and third pre-release: Fit to Space leaves hidden items alone, settles in one move, and
can keep an item towards a side. It passes the automated tests on Linux.

## Fixed

- **A fitted item that was hidden could come back somewhere else, on top of another item, and stay
  there.** The filter went on fitting the item while it was hidden, into the room the others had
  taken meanwhile. A hidden item is now left exactly where it is and holds no space. Shown again,
  it is where it was, and whatever took its room makes way for it.
- **Fitted items adjusted several times before they settled.** When an item is shown or hidden, or
  another Fit to Space says where its item is going, the new space is now taken at once, in one
  glide; only something that is on its way (being dragged, gliding, a window growing) is waited
  for. A scene that has just come into view is put right without a glide.
- **Copy transform: an item inside a group that is shown in several scenes** was moved inside its
  group by the copy tools. It is the same item in every scene and is now left alone.

## New

- **Fit to Space: *Keep it towards*** (*The middle* as it comes; the top, bottom, left, right or a
  corner). Where the item lies in its space when it does not fill it. Against a side, all the room
  left over is on the other side, so the item only ever moves that way: a camera kept towards the
  top stays at the top, and a chat box kept towards the bottom stays put when the camera above it
  is hidden.

## Please check

1. Hide and show the camera a few times in a scene where the camera and the chat box are both
   fitted: does the camera come back where it was, and does the chat box go back in one move?
2. Set *Keep it towards* on the chat box and the camera to the sides you want them to hold to.
