# StageKit 0.1.0-beta200

Scene Fit obeys Align in the space everywhere, the dock's Fit to space works with the filter on, and fitting no longer costs frame time while sizes change.

## New

- **Fit to space works while Scene Fit is on.** Press it on a source that its own fit filter resizes and that filter fits it at once: the source glides from where it is, a size you gave it by hand included, to fill its space. The status line says *Fitted by* and the filter's name.

## Changed

- **Align in the space is always obeyed.** A source lies in its space where its alignment says, also in a layout you made by hand with the filter off. Before, a source stayed where you had put it, so a chat box set to *The middle* could sit at the bottom of its space.
- **Switching the filter on can move a source.** If a source was not where its alignment puts it, it glides there when you switch the filter on. A layout made with the gaps and the alignments stays exactly as it is.
- **A quicker answer after a move or resize by hand.** About a third of a second after you let go, the filter takes over again: a source with *Resize to fit* on glides back to fill its space, from where you let go. It used to take a second or more, and a source beside it could take the room meanwhile.
- **Nothing happens while you hold a source.** On Windows the filter waits for the mouse button to be let go, however long you hold the source still. A button held in another window, a game say, does not count.

## Fixed

- **Higher frame times while a size changes.** A window switching, a Standby Image changing size, or a text that changes made every fit draw and read back what was beside it on every frame. That is gone: sources are looked at when they first appear and when a glance shows they draw something else.
- **Room kept for a source with no image.** A fitted capture that shows but has no image yet, and a widget with nothing to show for the moment, keep their room again. In beta198 the sources beside them moved in and jumped back.
- **Kept sizes read off a Standby Image.** A size was never meant to be read while a Standby Image stands in for a capture. A collection saved by an older version could have its kept sizes turned into the image's size at the first start after the update. They are now set when the capture's own image is there.
- **A kept-size source that shows more or less of itself.** A chat box or widget with *Resize to fit* off keeps its scale when it draws more lines. In beta198 it was squeezed into the size it had before.
- **A source hidden when the filter is switched on.** It keeps the size and space it had. Before, it was taken as it was at the moment a mode showed it, perhaps minutes later.
- **A source placed with empty margins.** A filter with no place for its source yet waits until it has looked at what shows of it, for a second and a half at the most, instead of placing a window with its empty margins and correcting it half a second later.
- **Fit to space under another source.** When the middle of the selected source lay on something in its way, the tool often found only a sliver of room. It now looks round the rest of the source too.
- **The kept-size line is back in the log.** `the size kept for ... went from ... to ...` says when and why a kept size was read, now in pixels.

## Good to know

- **A kept-size source set to The middle, above or below a camera.** One rule is unchanged: a source with *Resize to fit* off and *The middle*, lying above or below a resized image that has no use for more height (a camera as wide as its column), is kept the gap away from that image, not in the middle of the room left over. That came from an earlier request to keep the chat box 30 px from the camera. If you would rather have it in the middle there, say so; until then, choose a side or a corner for it.
- **Undo and the dock button.** Undo does not cover a press of Fit to space on a source its own filter resizes: the filter does the moving, and would fit an undone size again at once.
- **Holding a source on Mac and Linux.** The mouse button can only be read on Windows. Elsewhere the filter goes by the source having come to rest for a third of a second.
- **Stand-ins.** As in beta198, a Standby stand-in that shows in place of a hidden source is not in the way of fitted sources.
